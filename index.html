Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 29, 2026

Overview

HabitCraft is a habit tracking application built for personal use. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your information. We are committed to keeping things simple and transparent.

Information We Do Not Collect

HabitCraft does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or any other identifying details.

We do not use analytics services, advertising networks, or any third-party tracking tools. There are no ads in this application.

Data Stored on Your Device

All data you enter in HabitCraft, including habit names, icons, streak counts, and daily history, is stored locally on your device using Android's shared preferences storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

If you uninstall the app, all stored data is permanently deleted from your device.

Internet Access

HabitCraft does not require an internet connection to function. The app does not make any network requests. There is no data being sent to any server at any time.

Children's Privacy

HabitCraft does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Since no data is collected at all, this application is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, the new version will be made available within the app listing on the Google Play Store. We will update the effective date at the top of this page accordingly. Continued use of the app after any changes means you accept the updated policy.
